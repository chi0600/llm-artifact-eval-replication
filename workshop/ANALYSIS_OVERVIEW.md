# Workshop analysis: publication-safe overview

The manuscript uses qualitative content analysis of a cross-company workshop, with a deductive evaluation-problem path and an inductive mitigation-pattern path. The publicly shared codebook lists the 41 descriptive codes but does **not** contain original transcripts, segments, speaker identifiers, or human reviewer decisions.

## Evaluation-problem taxonomy

The eight provisional evaluation problems were defined from literature and prior observations, then reviewed against workshop evidence to confirm, qualify and contextualize them:

- E1 — Input quality variability
- E2 — Convincing but wrong outputs
- E3 — Non-determinism and reproducibility
- E4 — Oracle ambiguity
- E5 — Evaluating the evaluator
- E6 — Assistant-to-agent boundary and accountability
- E7 — Maintainability versus functional adequacy
- E8 — Scale and system-of-systems complexity

The manuscript reports a minimum threshold of 5 relevant segments from 2 interaction contexts for retention, and a high-support threshold of 10 relevant segments from 3 contexts; all eight provisional problems were retained.

## Mitigation patterns

The six mitigation patterns synthesized from workshop discussions are:

- M1 — Use-case criteria and human decision matrices
- M2 — Assistant-first risk framing
- M3 — Grounding through RAG/MCP and specialist routing
- M4 — Layered Swiss-cheese evaluation
- M5 — Tests as autonomy guardrails
- M6 — Continuous feedback and monitoring loops

The manuscript describes seven derived requirements (R1–R7) and a six-stage reference evaluation framework. This summary records the published analytic categories; it is **not** an independently auditable evidence table. Without the restricted statement-level data, external researchers cannot independently verify category support or recode the original discussions.
